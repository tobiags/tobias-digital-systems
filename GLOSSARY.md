# GLOSSARY

Canonical vocabulary for the **Tobias Agossou — Digital Systems Studio** web platform.

### Digital Systems Studio
The professional positioning of Tobias Agossou. Represents the fusion of strategic product design, web architecture, and automated workflow systems (IA & APIs).

### Warm Editorial Aesthetics
The visual identity language defined in the design specification:
- Background: Warm cream canvas (`#f6f3ee`)
- Typography: High-contrast pairing of serif display (*Bodoni Moda*), neutral grotesque body (*Inter*), and technical monospace (*JetBrains Mono*)
- Accent: Electric cobalt blue (`#2952ff`)
- Surfaces: Elevated cards with subtle border borders (`rgba(17,17,17,0.10)`) and delicate shadows

### Floating Drift Cards
Micro-animated interactive floating badges anchored around Tobias's portrait in the Hero section, displaying studio capabilities and key accomplishments (`driftA` / `driftB` oscillation).

### 4-Step Process
The client delivery methodology:
1. **01 Immersion** (Comprendre les enjeux, aligner la vision)
2. **02 Architecture** (Structurer les flux, définir le modèle de données)
3. **03 Conception & Prototypage** (UI haute fidélité, micro-interactions, retours)
4. **04 Déploiement & Optimisation** (Mise en production, tests, vélocité)

### Verified Review Proofs
Social proof assets displaying quantified client feedback from ComeUp and Fiverr (400+ avis 5 étoiles, 508+ missions, freelance depuis 2021).

### Tracer-Bullet Ticket
A discrete, independently testable vertical slice of the implementation that crosses from frontend component to rendering and verification.
